---
title: Why industrial autonomy is the real bottleneck for space colonization
date: 2026-06-15
tags: space, ai, industry
slug: industrial-autonomy
---

The hard part of settling another planet is building and running things where you can't send a technician: mining, manufacturing and maintaining systems somewhere a radio signal takes up to 24 minutes each way.

That's not a propulsion problem. That's an industrial autonomy problem. And we haven't solved it on Earth yet.
